Interior of a traditional Korean onggi pottery workshop in Jeonbuk, South Korea. Rows of large dark-brown glazed onggi earthenware jars stand along a whitewashed earthen wall. In the foreground a low wooden kick wheel holds a half-thrown wet clay jar; beside it a clay-dusted wooden workbench with simple wooden ribs and a bowl of water. Soft warm morning light through a small wooden-lattice window covered with hanji paper; warm off-white walls, earthy umber and ochre tones with a hint of olive green.
